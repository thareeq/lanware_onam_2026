📖 Competition Rules

Please follow the rules below for each competition. The organizers' decision will be final.

🏸 Badminton

  • Doubles competition.
  • Best of 3 games.
  • 15 points per game
  • BWF rules apply.

⚽ Football

  • 5-a-side football.
  • 15 minute halves.
  • Rolling substitutions.
  • Rules of fives games apply
  • The Back-Pass Rule apply
  • Three total games
  • Best two teams to Final
  • points awarded : Win: 3, Draw: 1
  • If same points then winner is based on number of goals
  • Referee decision is final
  • If any doubts on rules, Contact committee before the tournament

🎯 Carrom

  • 20 minutes play
  • If times exceeds winner is declared based on remaining coins
  • If equal coins then time is extended for 10 minutes
  • Standard Carrom Federation rules.
